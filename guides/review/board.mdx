---
title: "View daily work in Board"
description: "Board is an optional Operations view for daily jobs."
---

It helps an operations user see work for a selected day rather than search the entire dispatch history.

<Tip>
**Example:** An empty board for today may be correct even when last week's dispatches are still visible elsewhere.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Operations must be available in your workspace. A dispatch is a job or movement tracked by the Operations module.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Find the Operations area in the sidebar and click Board.
2. Read the selected date before interpreting the board.
3. Use the date control to select the day you want to inspect.
4. Choose a displayed view such as Kanban, Timeline or Map. Kanban organizes work in columns; Timeline emphasizes timing; Map emphasizes location.
5. Read the jobs shown for that day. To inspect work beyond the selected day, click Dispatches in the sidebar.
6. Use [Review dispatches and their next actions](/guides/review/dispatches) to investigate an individual job and its next action.

<Check>
**Expected result:** You know which day you are viewing and whether its job list explains the question you started with.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If the board is empty, check the date and compare with Dispatches before assuming all jobs are missing.
</Warning>
