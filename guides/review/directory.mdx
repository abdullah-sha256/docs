---
title: "Find people and equipment in Directory"
description: "Directory organizes the people, vehicles and customers used by Operations."
---

It helps you check an operational reference before working on a dispatch.

<Tip>
**Example:** A dispatch names a driver. The Directory is the place to look for the corresponding driver entry; an entry alone does not establish portal access.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Operations must be enabled. This section covers finding entries, not inviting people or changing access.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Click Directory under Operations in the sidebar.
2. Choose Drivers when looking for a driver, Trucks for a vehicle or Customers for a customer.
3. Find the relevant entry using the identifying information displayed in the list. Confirm it is the correct person, vehicle or customer.
4. If your question concerns a job assignment, return to Dispatches and inspect that job's displayed driver and route.
5. If an entry is missing or access is unclear, ask the Operations administrator to check setup and permissions.

<Check>
**Expected result:** You have identified the relevant directory entry and separated that information from a job assignment or access decision.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** Do not assume that a driver listed in Directory can sign in to a driver portal. Enrollment and access need their own confirmation.
</Warning>
