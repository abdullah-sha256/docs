This matters because a correct example does not prove that a whole period is complete, and different screens may count different things.

<Tip>
**Example:** Gia says there are no unmatched records. You still need to check Matches needing attention: a grouped item can have missing bank evidence or a delivery issue.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Keep the Gia response available and note its organization, dates and source names.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Read the exact claim. Identify whether it concerns one transaction, a list of examples, a total or the completion of an entire period.
2. Open the citation or source link beside the relevant statement. Check that the destination is the record or transaction the answer describes.
3. Compare the source date, amount, currency, counterparty and money direction. Open the original document when the question depends on an invoice or statement.
4. For completion claims, open Records → Browse → Matches and inspect Needs attention for the same scope. Also check Documents for validation work and Books for ledger delivery where relevant.
5. If the source and the answer disagree, ask Gia to explain that specific discrepancy and show its sources. Keep the unresolved item on your review list.
6. Base the next action on the underlying evidence and your review process. A response saying "done" is not a substitute for checking the actual state of the item.

<Check>
**Expected result:** You can identify which evidence supports the answer and which claims still need checking.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If a citation is missing, inaccessible or unrelated, ask for another source and inspect the relevant area directly. Do not infer that missing evidence proves a payment did not occur.
</Warning>
