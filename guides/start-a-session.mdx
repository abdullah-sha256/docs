It helps you find an item, see the evidence behind it and identify work that still needs attention. You do not need to understand every menu before starting.

<Tip>
**Example:** You want to check whether a supplier payment has an invoice attached. First confirm the organization, then find the payment and inspect its evidence.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Have the Nini link and sign-in account provided by your organization. This guide starts after you have access; invitation and sign-in recovery are separate processes.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Open the Nini link supplied by your organization and sign in with your assigned account.
2. Read the organization name at the bottom of the left sidebar. This identifies the workspace whose information you are viewing.
3. If you belong to more than one organization, click your name and organization at the bottom of the sidebar. Under Switch organization, find the organization you need. The current one is marked Current organization.
4. Select the intended organization, then check the name at the bottom of the sidebar again before uploading, connecting a source or reviewing records. If the organization is not listed, ask your administrator to check your membership.
5. Use the sidebar to open the area you need. Start with [Find your way around Nini](/guides/navigation) to learn the navigation. If you already have connected data, try the guided review in [Review your work from start to finish](/guides/review/end-to-end-review).
6. When you are finished, open the same account menu and choose Sign out if you need to end your session, especially on a shared computer.

<Check>
**Expected result:** You know which organization you are in and which task to start with.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If you cannot sign in or the organization is missing, contact the person who gave you access. Reconnecting a bank is not a sign-in fix.
</Warning>
