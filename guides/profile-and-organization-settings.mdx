Use it to locate the right control and understand who should manage it.

<Tip>
**Example:** Changing your viewing preference is different from changing an organization detail that other users share.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Available sections depend on your access. Some fields can save automatically, so inspect existing values without typing into them.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Click Settings, then Profile under You. Read the displayed name and email to confirm which account is signed in.
2. The Profile page says name and email are managed by your identity provider. Use your organization's sign-in administration process to correct them; this page is not an editable contact form.
3. Open Appearance for display choices. If Email intake is shown under You, use it to choose where documents sent from your email are routed; see [Route emailed documents](/guides/documents/email-intake).
4. Under Organization, open Details to inspect the shared organization information. Treat any editable field as a shared setting; the reviewed Details page saves changes automatically.
5. Use Integrations for connected sources. If Members or Billing & usage is present, use those sections for the corresponding administration with your organization's responsible administrator.
6. Developer access and Privacy are separate settings destinations. You do not need developer setup for the everyday review workflows in this guide.
7. For automatic posting, matching sensitivity or locked periods, go to Records → Matching settings, covered in [Read matching settings and locked periods](/guides/records/matching-settings). These are not personal appearance preferences.
