---
title: "Use document storage and other integrations"
description: "Document-storage connections provide another way to bring supporting files into Nini."
---

Other accounting and ERP connectors can add source information where they are enabled. Choose a connector because it supplies information you need, not simply because it is listed.

<Tip>
**Example:** If invoices are stored in a shared document service, a document connection may reduce repeated manual uploads. For a one-off file, Upload is simpler.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Confirm which source account owns the files or records and what access the connector requests. Do not assume a connection is limited to a folder: the reviewed material does not establish a working folder-only restriction for Google Drive.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Open Settings → Integrations → Add integration.
2. Find the provider. The reviewed catalog showed Google Drive, OneDrive and SharePoint for documents, plus Wave and Workday among the accounting and ERP options.
3. Open About to read the provider details. A Disabled label means it is not currently enabled; an Enable button is a setup action, not evidence that it is already importing.
4. If your organization has approved the source and access scope, start the displayed setup action and complete the provider's authorization prompts.
5. Return to Integrations and check the resulting connection state and Recent activity.
6. For document sources, open Documents → Browse and find a known file. Check destination and processing status before assuming it is ready for review.
7. For accounting or ERP sources, inspect an imported record against the source and confirm supported coverage with your administrator.

<Check>
**Expected result:** You know which connector is in use, what access was granted and whether the expected information has appeared.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If the requested permissions are broader than intended, cancel setup and ask your administrator to confirm the approved approach. For a limited set of files, use manual upload while access scope is being resolved.
</Warning>
