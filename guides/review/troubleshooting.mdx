This often explains missing items or different counts and gives your support contact enough information to investigate.

<Tip>
**Example:** A statement looks missing because the list is showing Analysis only while the file was uploaded for Reconciling.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Keep the relevant screen open. Note what you expected to see and what actually appeared.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Confirm the organization at the bottom of the sidebar and the area named at the top of the page.
2. Check the date range and active filters. For Documents, also check Destination and status. For Accounts, check the selected account and sync state.
3. Read the exact status or error. For a disabled Post control, inspect the review gate. For a Match, read evidence warnings and ledger delivery separately.
4. Open the original source or supporting record. Compare its date, amount, currency and description with the item in Nini.
5. Write down the steps that led to the problem, the expected result and the actual result. Include the period, filters and exact message.
6. Use your organization's agreed support contact if it remains unresolved. Share only the details needed to reproduce it; avoid repeated uploads, retries or reconnects.

<Check>
**Expected result:** You have either explained the difference or prepared a clear, reproducible question for support.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If Gia says work is complete, compare the live Needs attention queue for the same period. If Nini says Posted but QuickBooks has no new entry, check the current ledger-only posting boundary.
</Warning>
