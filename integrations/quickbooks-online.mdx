---
title: "Connect QuickBooks Online"
description: "The QuickBooks connection brings accounting information into Nini so you can compare it with bank activity and supporting documents, and ask Gia questions about the available information."
---

<Tip>
**Example:** Compare an imported supplier bill or bill-payment record with the bank payment and the invoice that supports it.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Use a QuickBooks account allowed to authorize the intended company. Confirm the Nini organization and make sure the company is not already connected. QuickBooks Desktop has a separate procedure in [Connect QuickBooks Desktop](/integrations/quickbooks-desktop).
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Open Settings → Integrations → Add integration.
2. Find QuickBooks. If it is already Connected, use Manage or its existing Connected card and check the linked company instead of creating another connection.
3. If it is not connected, use the setup action shown on the card. A disabled catalog entry may first offer Enable. Follow the provider connection flow opened by Nini.
4. Sign in through the provider's authorization screen. Select the intended QuickBooks company and review the requested access before granting it. The exact provider screens can vary.
5. Complete the provider prompts and return to Nini. Open the QuickBooks card and confirm the connection identifies the company you intended.
6. Wait for a sync status, then inspect recent activity and a sample record. Compare its date, amount, currency and counterparty with QuickBooks before treating the import as complete.
7. Use Records to review the imported information and Books to understand the current posting boundary. Posting in the reviewed Nini release writes to Nini's ledger, not back to QuickBooks.

<Check>
**Expected result:** The intended company is connected and you have checked that representative data has arrived.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If authorization finishes but records are missing, check sync status, dates and supported record types. Do not repeatedly disconnect and reconnect. Ask your administrator to investigate the existing connection.
</Warning>
