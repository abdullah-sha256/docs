---
title: "Choose the right source for your information"
description: "Connections bring information from other systems into Nini."
---

Bank feeds show money movement; accounting systems provide accounting records; document sources provide supporting files. You usually need more than one kind of evidence to explain an event.

<Tip>
**Example:** For a supplier payment, a bank feed can show the cash leaving the account, QuickBooks can provide the accounting record, and an invoice provides supporting detail.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Confirm the organization and identify the source account or company you intend to connect. Have the source owner available if you cannot authorize access yourself.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Open Settings → Integrations. Review Connected before adding anything so you do not start setup for a source that is already linked.
2. Read each connected card's sync status and Recent activity. Connected identifies an existing link; it does not by itself prove the latest data has arrived.
3. Click Add integration to open the catalog. Use About beside a provider to read its details. Availability and labels can differ by workspace.
4. For QuickBooks Online, use [Connect QuickBooks Online](/integrations/quickbooks-online). For QuickBooks Desktop, use [Connect QuickBooks Desktop](/integrations/quickbooks-desktop). Connect a bank from Accounts as described in [Connect a bank account](/integrations/bank-account).
5. For document-storage and other catalog options, use [Use document storage and other integrations](/integrations/document-storage-and-other). If you only need to add an individual file, follow [Upload a document to the right destination](/guides/documents/upload-a-document) instead.
6. After setup, check the connection and inspect a sample imported item using [Check connection health and imported data](/integrations/connection-health) and [View account balances and transactions](/integrations/account-balances-and-transactions).

<Check>
**Expected result:** You know which source supplies each kind of information and which setup route to use.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If a provider is missing, ask your administrator whether it is available for your workspace. A provider appearing in the catalog does not establish complete coverage of every feature in that system.
</Warning>

## Source choices

- Bank feed: balances and transactions; begin at Accounts → Connect bank.
- QuickBooks: the Online accounting connection; select QuickBooks in the integration catalog.
- QuickBooks Desktop: a separate connection with a Windows-machine handoff.
- Google Drive, OneDrive and SharePoint: document-storage options shown in the reviewed catalog.
- Wave and Workday: other accounting or ERP options shown in the reviewed catalog.
- Upload or email intake: ways to bring documents in without setting up a document-storage connector.
