---
title: "Connect a bank account"
description: "A bank connection supplies balances and transactions so Nini can show cash movement and compare it with accounting records and documents."
---

<Tip>
**Example:** Connect the operating account used to pay suppliers, then use its transactions as evidence when reviewing a supplier-payment Match.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Be authorized to connect the intended bank accounts. Know which organization and entity should own them, and have the bank's sign-in and verification method available.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Click Accounts in the sidebar, then Connect bank.
2. If Nini asks "Which entity is this bank for?", choose the intended entity. The reviewed dialog says its accounts and transactions will belong to that entity.
3. Click Continue to your bank. Complete the bank connection flow opened by Nini, selecting the correct institution and following its authentication prompts.
4. Review the accounts and requested access in the provider flow. Complete authorization only for the intended accounts, then return to Nini.
5. Find the resulting accounts in Accounts and check their names and masked numbers.
6. Read each account's balance date and sync state. A first sync may still be pending after the connection exists.
7. Open an account and compare a known transaction with the bank source. Use [View account balances and transactions](/integrations/account-balances-and-transactions) for the account-activity walkthrough.

<Check>
**Expected result:** You have the intended bank accounts in Nini and know whether their first data sync has completed.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If the bank or required account is unavailable, ask the connection owner for the supported route. If authentication expires, follow the connection's specific recovery instructions rather than creating another copy.
</Warning>
