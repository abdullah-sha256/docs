title: "Read Operations analytics"
description: "Analytics summarizes operational activity."
---

It helps you compare results only when the selected period, measure and cost coverage match the question you are asking.

<Tip>
**Example:** A displayed 100 percent margin may reflect missing operating costs. Check cost completeness before treating it as strong profitability.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Operations must be enabled. Know the period and measure you want to examine. Margin is a profitability measure whose meaning depends on the revenue and costs included.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Click Analytics under Operations in the sidebar.
2. Read the selected template and date range. Choose the available scope that matches your question.
3. Check the selected group and measure. These determine what is compared and what the chart counts or calculates.
4. Read the currency and labels. Confirm whether you are looking at an amount, count or percentage.
5. Check cost coverage for the jobs behind the result using their dispatch details. Do not interpret absent costs as zero.
6. Record the period, measure and coverage alongside any conclusion. Verify those details before using an export outside Nini.

<Check>
**Expected result:** You can describe what the chart measures and whether incomplete data limits its interpretation.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If the result seems implausible, check date range, measure and missing costs first. Ask for the calculation definition if it is still unclear.
</Warning>
